# Syntax Cheatsheet

Welcome to the Syntax Cheatsheet of Flint. This section is meant for everyone who already knows how to program, as this is just a collection of all the definitions, their syntax etc in Flint. It is a very condensed version of the three-part guide with only the bare minimum information in it. It mostly serves as a quick and easy way for you to look up syntax of stuff without going through the entire guides to find the information you need.

If you are not familiar with Flint but know how to program in general, feel free to read through the cheatsheet too to get a general feel of how Flint looks and whether you like it.
